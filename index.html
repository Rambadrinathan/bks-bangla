The West Bengal Wing

A platform for farmer dignity, knowledge and voice.

Bharatiya Krishak Samaj, West Bengal, is envisioned as a farmer-centred platform that listens first, organises patiently, and helps cultivators move toward resilient, profitable and ecologically balanced agriculture.

The Bengal wing will focus on training, awareness, farmer consultation, natural and chemical-conscious farming, seed sovereignty, indigenous inputs, value addition, market readiness and the confidence to speak with one voice.

National Legacy

BKS carries the spirit of the annadata.

The organisation stands for the Indian farmer’s right to dignity, fair policy, self-reliance, indigenous agricultural strength and practical knowledge that improves income without weakening soil, seed or community life.

Action Agenda

How BKS West Bengal can serve farmers.

Training and field seminars

Village-level sessions on profitable, sustainable, chemical-conscious and natural farming practices.

Seed and soil sovereignty

Awareness around native varieties, seed rights, soil health and the long-term security of Indian agriculture.

Organic and indigenous inputs

Promotion of organic manure, bio-inputs, local resources, natural methods and safer alternatives.

Farmer entrepreneurship

Helping farmers understand value addition, packaging, direct markets, farm tourism, urban demand and digital storytelling.

Policy voice

Structured feedback from farmers to public institutions on MSP, markets, inputs, water, crop choice and risk.

Youth and women in agriculture

Building pride and livelihood pathways for rural youth, women farmers and family-led farm enterprises.

West Bengal Opportunity

From fertile land to living prosperity.

West Bengal’s agriculture is diverse: paddy, vegetables, fruits, flowers, fisheries, livestock, wetlands, peri-urban farms and dense village economies. The opportunity is to connect this diversity with natural farming, training, aggregation, farmer-owned knowledge systems and better public representation.

Regenerative BengalSoil, water and biodiversity restoration through farmer-led practices.
Urban-rural food bridgesConnecting safe, local produce to Kolkata and Bengal’s growing cities.
Training at scaleUsing KarmYog’s learning systems to carry quality guidance to thousands of farmers.

AI for Annadata

Using artificial intelligence as a farmer’s practical companion.

BKS West Bengal will explore AI not as a distant technology, but as a local-language support system for farmers: helping them access timely knowledge, improve decisions, document field realities and connect better with markets, institutions and training networks.

Local-language advisory

Voice and WhatsApp-style guidance in Bengali, Hindi and simple English for crop care, soil health and natural input preparation.

Training at population scale

AI-assisted micro-lessons, videos, quizzes and field checklists to make expert knowledge available beyond one-time seminars.

Market and risk intelligence

Simple dashboards and alerts around weather, crop risk, demand trends, prices, aggregation and value-addition opportunities.

Farmer voice and documentation

Structured collection of farmer issues, success stories, field data and policy feedback so Bengal’s farmers can be heard with clarity.

Booth Volunteer Network

One farmer volunteer from every booth in West Bengal.

District leadership is only the top of the structure. The organisation itself is built booth by booth — one Booth Prabhari for each polling booth, who knows the farming families around them by name. Volunteers enroll for their own booth through a separate enrollment system.
