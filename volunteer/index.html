How it works

Why the booth, and not the district, is the real unit.

A district head cannot know ten thousand farmers. A booth volunteer knows three hundred, by name. The booth is the smallest unit where farmer problems are actually visible — which fields flooded, which family did not get their seed, which cold store cheated whom. BKS West Bengal builds upward from there.

01

Booth Prabhari

One per polling booth. Knows the farming families of that booth area, registers their issues, carries BKS training and information to them, and represents them upward.

02

Booth Sahayak

Up to four support volunteers per booth — youth, women farmers, agri-graduates. If your booth is already claimed, this is how you still serve it.

03

Gram Panchayat and Block

Booth Prabharis of an area form the panchayat and block committees. Nobody is appointed to a block seat without booth-level work behind them.

04

District and State

District leadership is chosen from those who built booth strength. The state committee under Mahacharya Sourabh J. Sarkar sits on this foundation, not above it.

What a Booth Prabhari actually does

Four hours a week. Real work, not a title.

  • Build and keep a living list of farming families in your booth area — what they grow, how much land, what they struggle with.
  • Carry BKS training on natural farming, seed, soil and input costs into your village, in Bengali.
  • Report farmer problems upward — crop loss, input price, mandi rates, irrigation, compensation delays — so the state unit can raise them with weight.
  • Bring at least ten farming families into BKS membership in your first ninety days.
  • Help women farmers and rural youth in your booth reach BKS training and enterprise programmes.

Booth enrollment

Find your booth. Check it. Claim it.

Your booth is identified by two numbers: your assembly constituency number (1 to 294) and your booth part number. Both are printed on your voter slip and on the EPIC roll. If you do not know them, ask at your gram panchayat office or your nearest BKS worker — and enroll anyway, we will help you fix the numbers.
